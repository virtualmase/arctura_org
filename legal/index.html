Legal

Scope, disclaimers, disclosure.

This page consolidates the boundary statements that otherwise appear inline across Council, ARMS, and Protocol.

What Arctura is not

No advice, no custody, no guarantees.

Arctura does not provide investment advice, payment guarantees, custody, automated trading, or professional (medical, legal, tax, or insurance) advice through any surface on this site.

Council

Council outputs are decision-support drafts, not professional advice. Named functions (Pauli, Planck, Lorentz) are AI functions, not personnel, and carry no autonomous authority. Consequential decisions require a named human approver.

ARMS

ARMS describes a conceptual authorization framework. It is not a custodial service, trading system, medical tool, or legal tool, and is not a license for unbounded autonomous agent behavior.

Protocol

Protocol is early-stage infrastructure. The current public record describes a single verified test transaction and an incomplete audit state. Protocol has not completed an independent security review. Treat it as building, not production-ready.

Security disclosure

Report vulnerabilities responsibly.

Report vulnerabilities to security@arctura.org. See /.well-known/security.txt for scope and disclosure timeline. Do not test mainnet contracts with funds beyond what you can afford to lose.

Archive content

Not the current record.

Academy and Signal Archives are retained as reference only and do not reflect current operations, positions, or record.
