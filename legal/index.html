Legal and boundaries

Know what this site establishes.

These statements keep educational material, institutional status, sponsored projects, and ecosystem operations from being confused with one another.

Last reviewed

August 30, 2026

Material changes to programs, legal status, data collection, or domain roles require this page to be reviewed again.

Education

Learning material, not professional authority.

ARCTURA materials are general educational resources. They are not engineering approval, professional licensure, legal, medical, tax, investment, financial, or security advice.

No accreditation claim

ARCTURA does not currently claim accredited courses, degrees, transferable credit, or recognized professional certification.

No outcome guarantee

Reading a pathway or completing an artifact does not guarantee competency, employment, ranking, revenue, safety, or system performance.

Independent review remains

Consequential technical and institutional decisions require qualified, accountable review appropriate to their context.

Institution and ecosystem

Distinct names, domains, and responsibilities.

Surface Current statement
ARCTURA Developing education organization at arctura.org. The site does not currently claim incorporated nonprofit or tax-exempt status.
Arctura Observatory ARCTURA-sponsored project preparing to migrate to arctura.space. Its tools structure user input but do not verify it.
Arctura Network Separate ecosystem initiative at arctura.network. ARCTURA pages do not grant Network roles, permissions, or authority.

Privacy

Static by default.

The current site does not require accounts. Observatory browser tools are designed to process entered text locally and do not send form contents to Arctura. External links are governed by their destination's policies. This statement must be updated before analytics, forms, enrollment, payment, or other data collection is introduced.

Security and correction

Report a problem specifically.

Report security issues to security@arctura.org and read the security policy. For inaccurate or stale public content, open a specific issue in the public repository with the affected route, proposed correction, and source when available.
