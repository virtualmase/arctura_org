Your working record

Tool contract

What this tool does—and does not do.

Inputs

A working question, intended outcome, evidence plan, boundary, owner, and review date.

Output

A plain Markdown brief that can be committed, shared, or expanded in any text editor.

Boundary

The tool structures what you enter. It does not research, verify sources, make decisions, or provide professional advice.
