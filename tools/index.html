Arctura Observatory / practice tools

Turn the question into a record.

Use free, browser-local tools to structure a complex question or claim before prose and automation blur the boundary. Your entered information stays in your browser.

Tooling posture

Structure before fluency.

Every tool should state its inputs, outputs, assumptions, and boundaries before it asks for trust.

Tool / 01

Signal Brief Builder

Turn a complex question into a concise Markdown brief with a scope, evidence plan, explicit boundary, accountable owner, and review date.

Private by default: the form runs only in your browser. Generated records are downloaded directly to your device.

Tool / 02

Claim Record Builder

Turn a stated claim into a reviewable Markdown record with a status, cited sources, an explicit boundary, an accountable owner, and a review date.

Private by default: the form runs only in your browser. It records your inputs; it does not verify them.

Tool contracts

What the tools do—and do not do.

Inputs

A working question or stated claim, evidence plan or cited sources, a boundary, an accountable owner, and a review date.

Outputs

A portable Markdown brief or claim record that can be committed, shared, or expanded in any text editor.

Boundary

The tools structure what you enter. They do not research, verify sources, make decisions, or provide professional advice.
