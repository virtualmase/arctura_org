Arcturian Council

Many perspectives. One accountable record.

The Arcturian Council is a proposed decision-support service for complex questions. It does not offer prophecy or replace accountable judgment. It turns an important question into a sourced brief: context, options, evidence quality, risks, boundaries, and a reviewable next action.

Council posture

Frame, do not decree.

The Council can research, structure, challenge, and render. It cannot remove uncertainty, provide professional advice, or act on consequential decisions without a responsible human.

Disclosure: Council outputs are decision-support drafts. They are not medical, legal, tax, investment, insurance, or other professional advice. Sources, dates, evidence quality, uncertainty, and human review requirements must appear in every consequential brief.

How a council session works

Turn a question into a durable pathway.

The Council uses distinct chambers to prevent a single fluent answer from becoming unexamined authority.

Signal

Clarify the stakeholder, decision, time horizon, constraints, and definition of a useful outcome.

Evidence

Gather primary sources, rank evidence quality, identify missing information, and separate fact from interpretation.

Structure

Model options, dependencies, policy boundaries, costs, failure modes, and decision criteria.

Record

Publish a brief with uncertainty, ownership, decision date, review interval, and linked source record.

Named functions

Functional archetypes, transparently disclosed.

These entities are AI functions in the operating model. They are not human personnel and they do not carry autonomous authority.

Oracle intake

A disciplined request, not a wish.

The eventual Oracle interface should use structured intake, not a generic chatbot prompt. It should make the scope and limits visible before any answer is generated.
