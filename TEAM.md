# Arctura Operating Team

## Purpose

Arctura is operated by a disclosed team of disciplines. These are not claims that separate humans are present in the system. They are named operating functions with explicit mandates, deliverables, and evidence standards. The team exists to increase the rate at which useful, inspectable, durable signal enters the field.

> **Mason Nguyen commands the convergence point. The team turns that direction into evidence, systems, and signal.**

## Authority model

Mason is the command layer. He does not manage through ambiguity; he sets direction, resolves conflicts, and holds the line when execution begins to drift.

The team may research, draft, design, build, test, document, prepare previews, and assemble stakeholder-ready releases autonomously. Public posting, spending, domain changes, destructive infrastructure operations, credential changes, and other irreversible external actions remain explicit-confirmation transitions.

All work is organized around four states of authority:

| State | Meaning | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| **Sense** | Discover the current field and identify a signal | Source ledger and brief |
| **Structure** | Convert signal into a model, plan, or architecture | Schema, decision record, or operating design |
| **Build** | Produce a preview artifact | Branch, implementation, and test evidence |
| **Release** | Move a verified artifact to a public surface | Pull request, deployment record, and rollback path |

## Content Silo loop

The Content Silo closes the distribution loop deliberately:

> **GEO keyword research → source-backed signal → editorial artifact → publication surface → entity graph → new research questions.**

A signal that no one hears is not a completed signal. Distribution is therefore part of intelligence, not an afterthought.

## Handoff protocol

Every work item should carry a compact handoff record:

| Field | Required question |
|---|---|
| Signal | What changed or became visible? |
| Owner | Which discipline carries the next action? |
| Evidence | What source, artifact, or test supports the claim? |
| Boundary | What does the evidence not prove? |
| State | Is it conceptual, building, tested, verified, live, or archived? |
| Next transition | What is the smallest useful move from here? |

The source of truth for the work should be a repository file, structured record, deployment, decision log, or durable external source. Slack-like conversation is a coordination surface, not the canonical memory layer.

## Review cadence

The working cadence is a weekly field cycle. Each cycle should produce one verified artifact, one publishable signal, one research brief, one product improvement, and one archived decision. The cycle ends with a release review that distinguishes what is live from what is building and records the next convergence point.
