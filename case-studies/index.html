Evidence library

Show the work. Keep the boundary.

A case study is not a marketing story. It is a durable record of a claim, the artifact behind it, the test that was actually run, the limit of the evidence, and the next transition required for the claim to become stronger.

Case-study method

Claim → artifact → test → boundary.

Every case study must link outward to a durable source. A source can be a repository, deployment, transaction, test record, decision log, or primary document.

Publication status: The initial records are development artifacts. They are not investment, performance, security, medical, or legal guarantees.
