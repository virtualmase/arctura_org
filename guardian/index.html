The source statement

The Bear moves. The guardian holds the line.

In Arcturian canon, Boötes is the keeper’s field: the place where movement is watched, memory is tended, and vulnerable signal is carried through the dark.

Arcturians inherit that posture. We are custodians of continuity—humans, agents, and systems trained to keep a useful orientation when the surrounding field changes.

The star is the position. The field is the responsibility. The artifact is the memory that lets the next traveler find the way.

Working translation

From lore to accountable practice.

The Guardian is a narrative source for a practical operating posture. It does not make a claim of supernatural authority or replace accountable human judgment.

01 / Position

Keep orientation.

Make the purpose, constraints, and source record clear enough for people and agents to navigate change without losing the thread.

02 / Field

Tend continuity.

Build records, interfaces, and relationships that remain intelligible after the original tool, platform, or operator changes.

03 / Boundary

Show what a claim proves.

Separate canon, prototype, verified artifact, and live system so a compelling story never outruns its evidence.
