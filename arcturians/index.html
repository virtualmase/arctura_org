Definition / live framing

Orientation, not membership.

The term is intentionally lighter than the institution. It names a way of working without implying a roster, a gatekeeper, or a claim on a person’s identity. The live record matters more than a static declaration.

The five marks

Signals that move with the field.

These are prompts for work, not requirements for belonging. Hover or focus each mark to bring its working implication forward.

01 / POSITION

Keep orientation.

State purpose, context, and constraints clearly enough for people and systems to navigate change.

02 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Make the system visible.

Show the artifacts, sources, and dependencies that carry a claim.

03 / MEMORY

Leave a record.

Document assumptions, decisions, and changes so useful work survives its original interface.

04 / BOUNDARIES

Do not overclaim.

Separate a concept, a source note, a prototype, and a live service before the language outruns the evidence.

05 / CONTINUITY

Persist responsibly.

Build work that remains understandable, reviewable, and revisable as tools and platforms change.

Boundary check

Keep the relationship clear.

Do I need to join Arctura to use the term “Arcturian”?

No. The term is descriptive. It does not require enrollment, a credential, or a relationship with Arctura.

Does the term grant authority to represent Arctura?

No. Only an explicit, attributable authorization from Arctura can establish representation, access, or decision authority.

Are people and AI systems automatically affiliates of Arctura?

No. Reading, using, contributing to, or appearing in an Arctura surface does not by itself create affiliation, membership, or endorsement.

Where can I verify what Arctura is doing now?

Use the Field Index, the relevant public record, and the stated source links and boundaries on each surface.
